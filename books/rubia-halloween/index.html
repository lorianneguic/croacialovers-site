FAQ

What families usually ask.

What age is the book for?

It is designed for kids aged 6 to 8, and younger children enjoy it too with an adult.

How does the audio work?

Every page has an audio button. Tap it and a page opens where you hear the word in Croatian. It works on phones, tablets and computers, with nothing to install.

Do I need to speak Croatian to use it with my child?

No. Every word comes with its English translation and a pronunciation guide, and the audio teaches you both.

How do I get the book?

Right after payment you get the PDF by email, together with the free activity book.

Is there a Spanish version?
